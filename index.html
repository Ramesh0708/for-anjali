01 — the letter

You asked for a little website.
So here is one.

“i just want someone to make me a little website for my birthday, or literally anything, just to make me feel special at least once.”

I saw that, and the line under it. Itna toh main bhi deserve karti hoon na? Haan. Itna toh. Aur isse thoda zyada bhi.

This isn’t a campaign and it isn’t a pitch. It’s a page that exists because you said, quietly, that you wanted to feel considered. You already do that for other people’s products — Arashiyama, JMarket, Utsav, Superteam Brazil — the dense grids, the slow-connection pages, the type that knows when to shut up. Somebody should hand a page back to you.

You call yourself “just a dev who makes web apps look and feel awesome.” The work is more specific than that. You design things that have to hold up in front of real users, then you build them. WeSee, Mumbai, late nights in the contribution graph. That’s a person, not a moodboard.

Happy birthday, whenever the day lands.
You deserve to feel special. At least once. And then again.

02 — what I noticed

Selected things,
kept on purpose.

  • travel experience

    Arashiyama

    A destination told like a story, with a path from looking to arriving. Built for people on slow connections, not just fast laptops.

  • marketplace

    JMarket

    Product-first, scannable, dense without becoming noise. The kind of grid that still feels calm on a phone.

  • fine jewellery

    Utsav

    Quiet type, generous space, photographs given room. Restraint is also a skill.

  • community

    Superteam Brazil

    A dark theme that still has hierarchy. Landing, members, ecosystem — aligned to someone else’s brand, and still yours in the craft.

Also on the shelf: Twenty Cents, Kaizen Arts, and a portfolio at anjali.monster that already knows how to speak in big type.

03 — a very small ritual

Light it. Make a wish.
The timeline can wait.

All three. That’s the whole ceremony. Wish for work that feels like yours, and a year that feels like it was made with you in mind.

Tap each candle.

Itna toh.

If this made you feel even a little bit special, it did the job. If it didn’t, the intention still stands — you were thought of, on purpose.
